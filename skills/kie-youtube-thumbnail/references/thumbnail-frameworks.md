# Thumbnail concept frameworks

Use these as ideation lenses, not formulas. Keep the promise truthful.

1. before versus after;
2. expected versus surprising result;
3. one impossible-looking but real moment;
4. clear mistake and consequence;
5. secret/reveal with visible evidence;
6. scale contrast;
7. tiny subject versus huge obstacle;
8. one expressive face plus one object;
9. product beauty plus unexpected environment;
10. simple number plus visual proof;
11. unfinished action;
12. binary choice;
13. transformation sequence compressed into one frame;
14. familiar object used unusually;
15. clean diagram or arrow-guided mechanism;
16. high-stakes deadline or constraint.

Score each concept from one to five for truth, immediate comprehension, curiosity, visual hierarchy, mobile readability, reference fidelity, and distinctness from the other variants. Reject any concept scoring below four on truth.
