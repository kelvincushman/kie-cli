# Likeness photo guide

Prefer eight to twelve JPEG, PNG, or WebP images, with five to twenty as the useful working range.

Include:

- front, three-quarter, and profile angles;
- close, medium, and wider framing;
- neutral, smiling, and natural expressions;
- indoor and outdoor light;
- current hair, facial hair, glasses, and distinctive features;
- single-person frames at useful resolution, ideally at least 1024 pixels on the short side.

Avoid:

- group photos, heavy filters, face swaps, or extreme retouching;
- sunglasses or obstructions in most images;
- near-duplicate frames from one burst;
- private documents, medical imagery, or unrelated background secrets;
- references without the subject's permission.

Record only the minimum consent context needed for responsible reuse. Do not store government identifiers, private contact details, or unrelated sensitive metadata in the profile name.
